Method

How an engagement runs.

Order matters here — each step exists to make the next one cheaper.

1

Map the ground

What systems exist, who owns them, and where the data actually lives. Usually a week, sometimes less.

2

Prove the seam

One narrow slice, end to end, against real data. If it doesn't work here, it won't work at scale.

3

Harden it

Auth, rate limits, logging, cost controls, and the failure paths nobody enjoys writing.

4

Hand it over

Documentation and a walkthrough for your team. The goal is that you don't need us afterwards.
